About

Faster than the tab you were about to open.

Highlight a word and get its definition. Highlight a paragraph and have it summarized, translated, or rewritten. Highlight a name and send it to six sites at once. Highlight a passage worth keeping and it's saved with a link back to exactly where you found it. One selection, and everything you might want to do with it is already there.

What it is

Swift Search is a Chrome extension. Highlight text on any page and a small menu appears — search it across the sites you choose, define it, translate it, summarize it, save it for later, or ask a question about it.

It works on every website, inside PDFs, and is completely free.

Not how most extensions work

A free extension is usually free because you're paying some other way. Swift Search isn't. No ads, nothing sold, no account needed for anything but the AI — and your sites, settings and highlights never leave your machine.

Who makes it

Swift Search is run by a tiny but dedicated team who are continuously working hard to improve the extension.

Get in touch
